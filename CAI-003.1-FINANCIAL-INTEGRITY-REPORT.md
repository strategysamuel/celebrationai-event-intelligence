### 5. FINANCIAL ENGINEERING RULES COMPLIANCE

The `celebrationIntelligenceEngine.ts` implementation enforces seven financial engineering rules:

1. **No Negative Category Allocations:** All 8 category allocations are strictly positive integers (`allocatedAmount > 0`).
2. **No Duplicated Expenditure:** Every expense item maps to exactly one category without double-counting.
3. **No Omitted Cost Categories:** All 8 standard event categories (Catering, Venue, Decoration, Photography, Accommodation, Transport, Miscellaneous, Entertainment) are represented in all options.
4. **No Hidden Deficit:** Option 1 and Option 2 reconcile to exactly ₹15,00,000; Option 3 explicitly reports the ₹3,75,000 deficit.
5. **Exact 100% Allocation Reconciliation:** Category allocations sum to `totalEstimatedCost` with zero floating-point remainder.
6. **Contingency Reserve Accounted For:** Miscellaneous & Contingency line item is explicitly included in all option budgets (5% in Option 1, 3% in Option 2, 4% in Option 3).
7. **Integer Rounding Consistency:** All currency calculations use deterministic `Math.round()` arithmetic.
