# CelebrationAI – Project Bible
**Release:** CAI-001 | **PromptWars 2026** | **Vertical:** Celebration & Event Planner

---

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy

Celebrations mark life’s most cherished milestones—weddings, milestone birthdays, anniversaries, and community gatherings. Yet conventional event planning is marred by fragmented spreadsheets, opaque vendor quotes, hidden costs, and rigid schedules that shatter at the first sign of unexpected change.

**CelebrationAI** was conceived as an intelligent, empathetic, yet mathematically rigorous partner. It replaces guesswork with **deterministic financial intelligence**, backward-scheduled execution gates, objective multi-factor vendor scoring, and dynamic replanning that gracefully adapts when budgets shrink or dates shift.

---

## 2. Key Personas & Use Cases

### Persona 1: The High-Expectation Wedding Host
- **Scenario:** Organizes a traditional wedding in Chennai for 300 guests with a budget ceiling of ₹15 Lakhs.
- **Pain Point:** Anxiety over food quality, auspicious muhurtham deadlines, and family guest transport.
- **CelebrationAI Value:** Locks 30% to authentic catering, backward-schedules venue and priest bookings 150 days out, and holds a 7% emergency contingency fund.

### Persona 2: The Milestone Celebration Planner
- **Scenario:** 30th Birthday Gala or 25th Silver Anniversary in Bengaluru for 120 guests with a budget of ₹3.5 Lakhs.
- **Pain Point:** Balancing rooftop ambiance, live music, and mixology cocktails without overspending.
- **CelebrationAI Value:** Interactive What-If simulation tests budget trims across entertainment and decor while protecting premier beverage quality.

### Persona 3: The Corporate & Community Event Organizer
- **Scenario:** Annual Leadership Summit or Festival in Hyderabad for 250 attendees with an ₹8 Lakh budget.
- **Pain Point:** Critical path dependencies (AV streaming, stage fabrication, keynote rehearsal).
- **CelebrationAI Value:** Dependency tracking flags blockers before they cascade into milestone delays.

---

## 3. Product Principles

1. **Deterministic Numbers, Creative AI:** AI generates ideas, drafts proposals, and mitigates risks; deterministic mathematical logic computes money, dates, and scores.
2. **Never Overwrite Without Consent:** Replanning presents side-by-side Before/After diffs and requires explicit user confirmation before touching the active plan.
3. **Radical Transparency:** Clear separation between estimated and actual costs; transparent 35/30/20/15 vendor scoring breakdown; prominent `[DEMO DATA]` labels on synthetic entries.
4. **Resilient Always:** Works seamlessly online with Gemini API and offline with deterministic heuristic fallbacks.
